01 / ABOUT

Researching the
stochastic side of networks.

My research interests lie in stochastic modelling and performance evaluation of communication networks.

I use analytical tools from queueing theory, renewal and Markov processes, and matrix-analytic methods to study delay, workload, and information freshness in systems with dependent or triggered arrivals.

My recent work focuses on triggered packet streams, in which a primary packet generates a secondary packet after a random delay. I am also interested in dependent requests in caching systems and their effects on delay and Age of Information.
